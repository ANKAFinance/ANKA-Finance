Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Information we collect

We collect account information (name, email), financial data you enter (accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, investments), and billing metadata from Stripe, Apple, or Google when you subscribe.

How we use data

Your data powers finance tracking features within your private workspace. We do not sell personal financial data.

Payment processors

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe (web), Apple App Store, or Google Play. Each processor has its own privacy policy governing payment data.

Data storage and security

Data is stored on secure servers with encryption in transit (HTTPS). You may export transaction data as CSV.

Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data. You can delete your account in the mobile app settings, or contact support@ankafinance.com for help.

Account deletion

When you delete your account, profile information and finance records you entered are removed from the active service database. Some limited billing, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records may be retained where required by law or payment providers.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@ankafinance.com. We will respond to requests related to account access, deletion, and data rights in a timely manner.
