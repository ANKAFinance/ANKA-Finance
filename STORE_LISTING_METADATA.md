## 📝 Full Description

```
ANKA Finance helps you take control of your personal finances with a clean, intuitive interface. Track every aspect of your financial life — accounts, transactions, budgets, savings goals, and investments — all in one place.

KEY FEATURES:

• Dashboard — Get an instant snapshot of your income, expenses, net worth, and monthly spending
• Accounts — Track checking, savings, credit cards, investments, and more with live balance updates
• Transactions — Log income and expenses with categories, accounts, and notes
• Budgets — Set monthly spending limits for any category and monitor your progress
• Goals — Define savings targets, track progress, and stay motivated
• Investments — Monitor your portfolio with holdings, watchlists, and allocation analysis
• Reports — Visualize your spending patterns with monthly and yearly reports (Plus)
• Multi-Currency — Support for USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD, JPY, SGD, AED, CHF
• Export — Download your transactions as CSV for tax or analysis
• Privacy First — Your data stays private. No bank connections, no data selling
• Secure — Face ID / biometric unlock supported

SUBSCRIPTIONS:

• Plus ($4.99/month) — Unlock advanced reports, investment tracking, and budget alerts
• Family ($9.99/month) — Everything in Plus, plus shared budgets and priority support

Download ANKA Finance today and take control of your financial future.
```

## 🧪 Google Play Closed Test Instructions (New Accounts)

1. Go to Google Play Console → Testing → Closed Testing
2. Create a new test track
3. Add the tester email addresses (minimum 12 required)
4. Share the opt-in link with testers
5. Run the test for minimum 14 days
6. After 14 days, apply for production access
7. Once approved, promote to production
